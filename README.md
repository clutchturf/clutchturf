## clutchturf here 👋

Coffee, code, repeat.

I build mobile apps in Flutter and React Native, usually for people who lose signal halfway through a form. Most of my time goes into the unglamorous half of that — sync queues, conflict handling, and screens that open fast on a five-year-old Android phone. The other half goes into motion: a sheet that tracks your thumb, a list that doesn't jump when data lands mid-scroll.
